Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- The list has a column of tabs. The first tab, Achievements, holds base game achievements only. Every mod with achievements has its own tab, in alphabetical order, with its done and total count.
- The Other mods tab is gone. MagicLib achievements and the Quality Perks milestones sit in the tab of the mod they come from.
- For modders: new tab column (empty: a tab named after your mod, any text: that tab, vanilla: the first tab) and Builder.tab. requiredMod takes & between groups: a|b&c|d means (a or b) and (c or d).
- For modders: new built-in check industry:<id>, with optional AI core and special item: industry:<id>:<core>:<item>.
- When one check completes more than three achievements, three messages show and one line counts the rest. The file in saves/common is written once for all of them.
- Fixed: a built-in check with a dot in its key, such as memory:$my.key, was taken for a class name and the row was left out.
- The intel entry lists the counts per tab. An achievement's intel entry names its tab.
- Licence: CC0 1.0, in a LICENSE file.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-10)
- First public release.
- Achievements entry on the intel screen, with a button that opens the list.
- Every achievement starts greyed out with a hint. Once done it shows its name, what it was for and what it gave. A LunaLib setting shows the exact conditions instead of the hints.
- Achievements are kept across all saves. Counted ones show their progress in the current save.
- Chains: steps that show up one after another.
- A campaign message and an intel log entry when an achievement is done. A LunaLib setting turns the message off.
- 10 achievements for the base game.
- Other mods can add achievements with a CSV file and a small Java API.
- With MagicLib installed, the Other mods tab lists the achievements of MagicLib mods such as Nexerelin. It also lists the milestones of Quality Perks and Quality Captains.
