Version 0.5.0 (2026-11-12)
- The list opens from the new Achievements button on the ability bar, or with a hotkey (K, set in LunaLib). The Achievements entry on the intel screen is gone.
- The intel entry of a new achievement has a Show it in the list button that opens its tab, scrolled to it and marked.
- Every achievement shows its name, with the hint under it until it is done. Secret ones read Secret Achievement instead of ???. A chain step not reached yet reads Next step.
- MagicLib achievements sit among the others in their mod's tab, without a MagicLib heading.
- What an achievement pays or unlocks in other mods shows under it, done or not: Rule Book rules, and New Game Plus career points once New Game Plus supports it.
- Chains have more space around them and padding below the last step.
- Achievements that can be done in any order are no longer chains: the colony ones, the Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal ones, Destroy a shrouded ship (Sol Renewed), and the quests of Nexerelin, Sephira Conclave (Missing Aces, False Flag Operation) and Emergent Threats.
- Enter 25 star systems is replaced by a chain: visit 25%, 50% and 100% of the Sector's star systems in one campaign. Hidden systems do not count.
- Defeat an Omega, Threat or Dweller fleet is now three achievements: Defeat a Shrouded Dweller fleet (it keeps the Rule Book unlock), Defeat an Omega fleet and Defeat a Threat fleet.
- For modders: Achievements.addRewardText adds a line under achievements (what your mod gives for them), and Achievements.openList(ui, id) opens the list at one achievement.

Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-11)
- 157 achievements for the quests, story missions and named bounties of 37 other mods, such as Nexerelin, Secrets of the Frontier, Sephira Conclave, Crab Shack, Domain Phase Lab and Emergent Threats. Each sits in the tab of its mod and shows only while that mod is on.
- The New Game Plus - Renewed feats about other mods are among them, with the same names, so they also show without New Game Plus and count once.
- For modders: the memory, player_memory and persistent checks take several keys ($a&$b, $a|$b), a value ($key=VALUE, $key>=10) and !$key for not set. New checks person_memory:id:$key, faction_memory:id:$key, entity_memory:id:$key and skill:id.

Version 0.3.2 (2026-11-11)

Version 0.3.1 (2026-11-11)

Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-11)
- 45 more achievements, taken over from Rule Book - Renewed with the same names and conditions: 26 for the base game (story missions, colonies, a million credits, battles) and 19 for other mods, each in the tab of its mod. Achievements you already have stay done.
- Their reward lines, such as "Unlocks the rule Ludd's Song", show only while Rule Book - Renewed is on.
- For modders: new column rewardMod. The reward text shows only while that mod is on (same form as requiredMod).

Version 0.2.1 (2026-11-10)

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- The list has a column of tabs. The first tab, Achievements, holds base game achievements only. Every mod with achievements has its own tab, in alphabetical order, with its done and total count.
- The Other mods tab is gone. MagicLib achievements and the Quality Perks milestones sit in the tab of the mod they come from.
- For modders: new tab column (empty: a tab named after your mod, any text: that tab, vanilla: the first tab) and Builder.tab. requiredMod takes & between groups: a|b&c|d means (a or b) and (c or d).
- For modders: new built-in check industry:<id>, with optional AI core and special item: industry:<id>:<core>:<item>.
- When one check completes more than three achievements, three messages show and one line counts the rest. The file in saves/common is written once for all of them.
- Fixed: a built-in check with a dot in its key, such as memory:$my.key, was taken for a class name and the row was left out.
- The intel entry lists the counts per tab. An achievement's intel entry names its tab.
- Licence: CC0 1.0, in a LICENSE file.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-10)
- First public release.
- Achievements entry on the intel screen, with a button that opens the list.
- Every achievement starts greyed out with a hint. Once done it shows its name, what it was for and what it gave. A LunaLib setting shows the exact conditions instead of the hints.
- Achievements are kept across all saves. Counted ones show their progress in the current save.
- Chains: steps that show up one after another.
- A campaign message and an intel log entry when an achievement is done. A LunaLib setting turns the message off.
- 10 achievements for the base game.
- Other mods can add achievements with a CSV file and a small Java API.
- With MagicLib installed, the Other mods tab lists the achievements of MagicLib mods such as Nexerelin. It also lists the milestones of Quality Perks and Quality Captains.
