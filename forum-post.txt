[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Achievements for the campaign, kept across all your saves.
Each one starts greyed out with a hint. Once you get it, it shows what it was and what it unlocked.
The base game has its own tab, and so has every mod that adds achievements.
Other mods can add their own achievements and chains with one CSV file.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How it works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Open the list from the Achievements entry on the intel screen.[/li]
[li]The first tab, Achievements, holds the base game's achievements. Every mod with achievements has a tab of its own, in alphabetical order.[/li]
[li]An achievement you do not have shows a hint. The LunaLib setting "Show how to get achievements" shows the exact condition instead, secret ones included.[/li]
[li]Counted achievements show your progress in the current save, for example "3 / 10".[/li]
[li]Chains come in steps. A later step shows up when the one before it is done.[/li]
[li]Getting one shows a message on the campaign screen and adds an entry to the intel log. A LunaLib setting turns the message off.[/li]
[li]Done achievements count for every save.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Works with[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Rule Book - Renewed[/b]: some achievements unlock its rules, and say which once done. Its challenges have a tab of their own.[/li]
[li][b]New Game Plus - Renewed[/b]: its feats are achievements here, and every achievement pays career points there, once per profile.[/li]
[li][b]MagicLib[/b]: the achievements of MagicLib mods (Nexerelin and others) are listed read-only, each mod in its own tab.[/li]
[li][b]Quality Perks, Quality Captains[/b]: their milestones are listed read-only in their own tab.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]For modders[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Add achievements with data/config/achievements_renewed/achievements.csv in your mod: name, hint, how-to line, icon, category, goal, secret, reward text, chain and step.[/li]
[li]Your achievements get a tab with your mod's name. The tab column puts a row in any other tab instead.[/li]
[li]Check them without Java: a memory flag, the player's level, colonies, credits, battles won against a faction, or an industry on a colony with a given AI core and item. Or name a Java class.[/li]
[li]Java API: isDone, progress, complete, addProgress, a listener for new achievements, and a call that opens the list.[/li]
[li]Sector memory holds $acr_doneCount and $acr_totalCount for rules.csv.[/li]
[li]The README on GitHub has the columns and examples.[/li]
[/list]
