Achievements - Renewed
by Oddisz

The screen's drawing helpers come from Oddisz's own Gambling Den and Rule Book - Renewed.
The base game achievements use icons that ship with Starsector, by path. The trophy icon is the mod's own.

MagicLib (Wispborne and the modding community, MIT licence): when MagicLib is enabled, its achievements are
read through its public classes and listed read-only. No MagicLib code or art is included.

Quality Perks and Quality Captains (Dal): their milestones are listed read-only from the game's own skill
list. No code or art of those mods is included.
